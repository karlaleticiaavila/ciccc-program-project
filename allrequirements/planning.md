# Who Are You Becoming?

## Problem Statement

Many professional platforms focus on achievements, job titles, and credentials, but rarely show the experiences, resilience, mentorship, and growth behind them.

## Project Objectives

- Help users document their personal and professional journey.
- Connect skills to real-life experiences and evidence.
- Allow mentors to validate skills and provide feedback.
- Create a more human alternative to traditional resumes.

## Target Users

- Students
- Career changers
- Mentors
- Recruiters
- Professionals building a portfolio

## Competitor Analysis

LinkedIn
- Strong professional network
- Focuses on jobs and credentials

Coursera
- Focuses on learning and certifications

Who Are You Becoming
- Focuses on personal growth, milestones, mentorship, and skill evolution

## MVP Features

- Authentication
- User Profiles
- Timeline Creation
- Milestone Management
- Skill Tracking
- Evidence Upload
- Comments
- Notifications
